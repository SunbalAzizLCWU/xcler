"What does an AI chatbot cost?" gets answers from "free" to "six figures". Both can be true — they describe different products. This guide breaks down what you actually pay for, so you can compare quotes properly.

All figures below are **typical project ranges we see in the EU market in 2026**, not fixed prices. Your scope decides where you land.

![AI chatbot cost tiers: Starter FAQ bot, Business RAG + CRM, Enterprise multi-agent](/blog/blog-ai-chatbot-cost-tiers.webp)

## The three tiers

### Starter: FAQ and lead capture bot

A chatbot that answers a fixed set of questions from your website and collects contact details. Often built on a SaaS widget with an LLM behind it.

- **One-off setup:** roughly €1,500 – €5,000
- **Monthly:** €50 – €300 (platform plus model usage)
- **Good for:** small businesses, landing pages, simple lead qualification

### Business: RAG assistant with integrations

The bot answers from **your own documents** — manuals, policies, product data — using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and connects to your CRM, ticket system or calendar.

- **One-off setup:** roughly €8,000 – €25,000
- **Monthly:** €200 – €1,500 depending on traffic and hosting
- **Good for:** customer support deflection, internal knowledge assistants, booking flows

### Enterprise: multi-agent systems

Several specialised agents (support, sales, order status, escalation) with tool access, audit logs, role-based access, multiple languages and SLA-grade monitoring.

- **One-off setup:** €30,000 and up
- **Monthly:** €1,500+ including monitoring and continuous improvement
- **Good for:** high-volume service desks, regulated industries, multi-country operations

## What drives the price

1. **Knowledge sources.** Ten FAQ answers are cheap. Two thousand PDFs with tables, versions and access rights need a real ingestion pipeline.
2. **Integrations.** Every system the bot reads from or writes to — CRM, ERP, helpdesk, calendar — adds build and test time.
3. **Languages.** German and English with correct tone in both is more work than one language.
4. **Compliance.** EU hosting, a data processing agreement, consent handling and deletion rules. See our [GDPR chatbot checklist](/en/blog/gdpr-compliant-ai-chatbot).
5. **Quality assurance.** Test sets, evaluation runs and human review before go-live. Cheap quotes usually skip this.
6. **Handoff to humans.** A clean escalation path to your team is what makes customers trust the bot.

## Running costs people forget

- **Model usage (tokens).** Usually modest for support bots, but it scales with traffic and long documents.
- **Vector database and hosting.** Small for most SMEs; grows with document volume.
- **Content upkeep.** When your prices or policies change, the knowledge base must change too.
- **Monitoring.** Someone should read conversation logs weekly and fix wrong answers.

A useful rule: budget **15–25% of the setup cost per year** for maintenance and improvement.

## Build on a platform or custom?

SaaS chatbot builders are fast and cheap to start, but you rent their limits: data location, integrations and pricing per conversation. A custom build on an automation stack such as n8n costs more upfront and gives you control over data, models and logic. For many mid-sized companies a hybrid — custom retrieval and integrations, off-the-shelf chat widget — is the sweet spot.

## How to compare quotes

Ask every vendor the same five questions:

1. Where is the data hosted, and who is the processor?
2. How is answer quality measured before and after launch?
3. What happens when the bot does not know the answer?
4. Which monthly costs are included, and which scale with usage?
5. Can we switch the language model later without rebuilding?

If a quote cannot answer these, the low price is not a saving.

## FAQ

**Can I get an AI chatbot for free?**
Free tiers exist, but they rarely include your own knowledge base, integrations or EU data processing.

**How long does a business chatbot take to build?**
A starter bot takes one to two weeks. A RAG assistant with integrations usually takes four to eight weeks.

**Does a chatbot replace support staff?**
It removes repetitive questions so your team handles the complex cases. The best results come from bot plus human, not bot instead of human.
