Ask five agencies in Germany for a website quote and you may get prices from €2,000 to €60,000 for what sounds like the same brief. The spread is not random. It reflects very different amounts of strategy, design, custom development, content and SEO.

The ranges below are **typical 2026 project prices we see for professional agencies in Germany**. Freelancers and templates can be cheaper; enterprise projects can be far more.

![Website cost breakdown: strategy, design, development, content, SEO, hosting and care, with price bands in euros](/blog/blog-website-cost-breakdown.webp)

## Three typical project types

### Landing page or small site: €2,500 – €6,000

One to five pages, a contact form, basic SEO setup, responsive design, often built on a proven template or component system. Good for a new service, a campaign or a small business.

### Corporate website: €8,000 – €25,000

Ten to forty pages, custom design, multilingual (for example German and English), CMS for your team, integrations like CRM or newsletter, structured data, performance optimisation and a proper launch with redirects.

### Platform or web app: €25,000 and up

Customer portals, configurators, calculators, member areas, booking systems, headless shops or deep integrations with ERP and automation. Priced by scope and usually delivered in phases.

## Where the money goes

A typical corporate project splits roughly like this:

- **Strategy and concept — about 10%.** Goals, audiences, sitemap, keyword research.
- **Design — about 15%.** Wireframes, visual design, design system.
- **Development — about 40%.** Front end, CMS, forms, integrations, performance.
- **Content — about 10%.** Copywriting, translation, images.
- **SEO — about 10%.** Technical SEO, metadata, structured data, redirects.
- **Hosting and care — about 15%** of the first year, depending on the plan.

## What pushes the price up

1. **Custom design** instead of a template.
2. **Multiple languages** with properly localised URLs and hreflang.
3. **Integrations** with CRM, ERP, booking or payment systems.
4. **Custom features** — calculators, portals, search, filters.
5. **Content creation** if the agency writes and translates for you.
6. **Legal requirements** in Germany: imprint, privacy policy, cookie consent, accessibility where required.

## Running costs per year

- **Hosting:** from almost nothing for static sites to a few hundred euros for managed setups.
- **Domain and email:** small.
- **Maintenance:** updates, backups, monitoring — often €50 – €300 per month.
- **Content and SEO work:** optional but where growth comes from.

A good rule is to budget **10–20% of the build price per year** for care and improvement.

## How to compare quotes

Ask every agency:

1. What exactly is included — pages, languages, integrations, content?
2. Which technology do you use, and why for our case?
3. How do you handle SEO and redirects at launch?
4. What does maintenance cost, and what happens if we leave?
5. Who owns the code, design files and content?

A cheap quote that leaves out SEO, redirects or maintenance often becomes the expensive option six months later.

Templates are fastest and cheapest. WordPress suits content-heavy sites with active editors. Next.js is the choice when speed, SEO and custom features matter. We compare them in detail in [Next.js vs WordPress](/en/blog/nextjs-vs-wordpress-business-website).

## FAQ

**Why are agency prices higher than freelancers?**
You pay for a team — strategy, design, development, SEO — plus project management and continuity if someone is on holiday.

**How long does a website take?**
Landing pages take two to four weeks, corporate sites six to twelve weeks, platforms longer and often in phases.

**Can we start small and grow?**
Yes. A solid technical base lets you start with core pages and add features later without rebuilding.
