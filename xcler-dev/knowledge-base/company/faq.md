## What does an AI automation agency like XCLER deliver?

XCLER delivers AI chatbots, RAG agents and autonomous assistants integrated into a client's support and sales stack, plus n8n and Make.com workflow automation. XCLER ships production systems with guardrails, evaluation and human handoff — not demo chatbots without integration.

Yes. XCLER works as an n8n agency and Make.com agency. XCLER builds CRM sync, lead routing and operations workflows with retries and alerting for teams in Germany, DACH and the US. XCLER also implements Zapier and GoHighLevel when they fit better.

## Which web and app technologies does XCLER use?

XCLER primarily uses Next.js, React, TypeScript and modern APIs for websites and SaaS products. For commerce and content, XCLER uses Shopify and WordPress (including WooCommerce and headless setups). Backends and AI services are commonly built with Python (FastAPI, Flask) or Node.js; mobile apps with React Native or Flutter. Automation runs on n8n, Make.com, Zapier or GoHighLevel.

## How fast can a project with XCLER start?

XCLER typically kicks off discovery within a few business days after scope alignment. The client gets a clear plan, milestones and a single engineering team accountable for delivery.

## How do AI agents and workflow automation work together at XCLER?

XCLER connects AI agents to the automation layer: a chatbot qualifies a lead or understands a request, then n8n or Make.com routes the work into the CRM, Slack, helpdesk or operations queues. The AI handles understanding; the workflow handles deterministic actions. This combination is XCLER's core strength for B2B operations.

## What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions in a guided conversation. An agent takes actions — creating tickets, updating CRM fields or triggering n8n / Make.com workflows. XCLER builds both, with clear permissions and audit trails for every action an agent can take.

## How does XCLER keep AI answers safe and on-brand?

XCLER defines allowed tools, topic boundaries, escalation rules and evaluation sets for every assistant. XCLER monitors failure modes after launch and always keeps a human handoff path for sensitive cases.
