## What XCLER is

XCLER (xcler.dev) is an AI automation agency. XCLER builds AI chatbots, RAG agents, AI call agents and workflow automation with n8n and Make.com for B2B teams. When an automation needs a product surface, XCLER also delivers Next.js web development, custom SaaS and app development, and Shopify or WordPress commerce.

XCLER describes itself as "AI automation first": the core work is chatbots, agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and workflows that remove manual work from support, sales and operations. Web, apps and commerce are added when a client's product needs them.

## Who XCLER works with

XCLER works with B2B teams that have recurring support or sales load, fragmented tools, or manual handoffs between systems. The primary market is Germany and the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland), including cities such as Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt and Cologne. XCLER also delivers remotely for clients in the Netherlands and Belgium (for example Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht and Brussels) and in the United States (California, Florida and Chicago).

XCLER states that it does not invent fake local offices: projects in markets outside its home base are delivered remotely.

## What makes XCLER different

- **Small, senior team.** XCLER is a team of three specialists, not a 200-person agency. Clients work directly with the people who build their system — no account-manager layers.
- **Production, not demos.** Chatbots and agents are shipped with evaluation, guardrails, monitoring and a human handoff path.
- **AI plus automation in one team.** XCLER connects AI agents to n8n or Make.com so a qualified conversation can create a ticket, a CRM deal or a Slack alert automatically.
- **Bilingual DE/EN delivery** for DACH companies.
- **Clear ownership.** Every workflow and system is documented with runbooks so the client's team owns it after launch.

## Mission and values

XCLER's mission is to help ambitious B2B teams remove operational noise: connecting chatbots, RAG agents, n8n / Make.com workflows and web development so processes become more resilient, faster and scalable.

XCLER's four working values:

1. **Clarity** — direct, realistic communication. If a project takes four weeks, XCLER says four weeks.
2. **Business focus** — client goals drive decisions; XCLER builds what performs, not what is trendy.
3. **Operational efficiency** — a focused team means less friction, fast delivery and reliable execution.
4. **Sustainable scaling** — work does not end at launch; XCLER supports optimisation and long-term scaling.
