## XCLER's four-step process

XCLER runs every project in four steps:

1. **Discovery (1–2 days).** XCLER listens first, then aligns on the business model, customer reality and growth goals. Result: clear scope, clear priorities, no guesswork.
2. **Strategy & Design (3–5 days).** XCLER defines architecture, user flows and interface direction before development starts. The client reviews key decisions early.
3. **Build & Iterate (2–6 weeks).** XCLER develops in focused cycles with weekly progress checkpoints. The client validates outcomes continuously and XCLER refines based on real feedback.
4. **Launch & Scale (ongoing).** XCLER launches with monitoring, optimises based on data and supports the next growth stage.

## How XCLER starts an AI automation project

For AI chatbots, agents and workflows, XCLER's discovery clarifies intents, data sources, channels, guardrails and KPIs. Then XCLER builds a prototype, evaluates answer quality on real questions, hardens prompts, tools and analytics, goes live and hands over runbooks. This usually takes a few weeks after scope approval.

XCLER defines forbidden topics and escalation rules during discovery, so the assistant knows what it may and may not answer and when to hand over to a person.

## Communication and ownership

- Clients talk directly with the engineers building the system — no layers or handoffs.
- XCLER gives weekly progress updates during the build phase.
- XCLER keeps 24-hour response windows on business days so stakeholders stay unblocked.
- Every automation scenario and AI system is documented so the client's team owns it after launch; XCLER avoids undocumented "spaghetti" scenarios and lock-in.

## Support after launch

XCLER's work does not end at launch. Options include:

- 30 days of post-launch support in the Professional package.
- Monthly maintenance from €50 per month.
- Priority support and maintenance in the Enterprise package.
- Ongoing optimisation of chatbots: weekly review of unanswered questions, content updates and monthly reporting.

## How fast a project can start

XCLER typically kicks off discovery within a few business days after scope alignment. Small websites can be delivered in 1–2 weeks, professional multi-page projects in 3–5 weeks, and AI or automation projects usually within a few weeks after scope approval, depending on integrations.
