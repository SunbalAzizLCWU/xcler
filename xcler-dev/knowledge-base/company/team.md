## Musharraf Aziz — Founder & AI Automation Engineer

Musharraf Aziz is XCLER's founder and automation lead. At XCLER he leads AI automation, AI chatbots and agents, and workflow automation. His expertise is workflow automation, AI agents and RAG systems; his tools include Make.com, n8n, Zapier, GoHighLevel and AI call agents. He designs automation systems that reduce manual operations and improve execution speed at scale, and has 3+ years of experience turning manual operations into automation.

Musharraf also has a personal portfolio at https://maziz.me with his full engineering background — see the separate profile document "Musharraf Aziz".

## Abeel Mehr — Full Stack Engineer

Abeel Mehr is XCLER's full stack developer and leads web development and app development. His expertise is web and app development, API architecture and CI/CD; his tools include Next.js, React, Python, FastAPI, Flask and Docker. He builds web and mobile applications designed for speed, maintainability and scale, with clean architecture and production-grade code.

## Mehru Seemab — CMS & Frontend Engineer

Mehru Seemab is XCLER's CMS and frontend developer and leads the WordPress and Shopify service. Her expertise is WordPress, Shopify and e-commerce; her tools include WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Liquid and PHP. She specialises in CMS and e-commerce solutions that teams can manage with confidence, combining conversion logic, technical precision and organic visibility. Mehru also authored XCLER's Shopify pricing guides on the blog.
