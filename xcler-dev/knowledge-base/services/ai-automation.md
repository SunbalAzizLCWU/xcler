## How AI automation differs from a simple chatbot

A chatbot answers questions. AI automation connects answers to actions: creating tickets and deals, routing requests and triggering follow-ups through n8n or Make.com. XCLER prioritises use cases by business impact — ticket deflection, lead qualification, inbox routing and operations handoffs — rather than demo chatbots that are not integrated with anything.

## How XCLER builds AI automation

1. **Discovery:** intents, data sources, channels, guardrails and KPIs.
2. **Build:** chatbot or agent with RAG, tool calling and escalation rules.
3. **Orchestration:** once the intent is clear, n8n or Make.com runs the deterministic steps into CRM, Slack, helpdesk and billing.
4. **Evaluation:** answer quality is tested against real questions before go-live.
5. **Go-live and runbooks:** monitoring, documentation and ownership handover.

This usually takes a few weeks after scope approval.
