## What XCLER's AI chatbots and agents service is

These assistants answer from a company's own knowledge base, qualify leads, answer customers and hand off to humans cleanly. ## What XCLER's chatbot and agent capabilities are

- **RAG-powered chatbots:** assistants grounded in the client's documents, policies and product knowledge.
- **AI call agents:** voice agents for call handling, qualification and appointment scheduling.
- **Customer support bots:** automated coverage for repetitive questions, with human handoff when needed.
- **Lead qualification:** AI conversations that detect intent and route high-fit leads to the sales team.

## XCLER's typical AI chatbot architecture

XCLER's typical architecture: document ingestion and embeddings, retrieval with citations, LLM orchestration with tools, evaluation harnesses and escalation to humans. Agents are connected to workflow automation (Make.com or n8n) so qualified intents become tickets, deals or operations tasks automatically.

XCLER's AI engineering covers prompt design, retrieval pipelines, evaluation harnesses, guardrails, analytics and human takeover. XCLER combines LLM providers, embedding stores, Next.js operator interfaces and automation bridges.

## How XCLER keeps chatbots accurate and safe

1. **RAG knowledge architecture:** LLMs connect to curated, versioned knowledge sources with retrieval controls, so answers follow the client's real policies.
2. **Controlled tool access:** agents act through explicit permissions and bounded actions when connected to CRM, ticketing or scheduling.
3. **Topic boundaries and escalation rules** are defined in discovery, including forbidden topics.
4. **Evaluation and monitoring:** XCLER measures answer quality, intent accuracy, escalation behaviour and business outcomes, then improves prompts, retrieval and workflows over time.
5. **Human takeover** is always available for sensitive cases.
