## Why XCLER emphasises SEO and performance

Many business websites lose rankings because technical execution is inconsistent, not because content is weak. XCLER treats Core Web Vitals and technical SEO as architecture requirements, enforced through QA and deployment checks so improvements last after launch.

## How XCLER protects rankings during a relaunch

XCLER prevents SEO loss with structured migration planning: URL mapping, 301 redirects, canonical continuity, internal link repair and staged validation in Google Search Console. XCLER treats a relaunch as a controlled search and data migration, not just a visual redesign.
