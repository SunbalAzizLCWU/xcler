Pages: https://xcler.dev/en/services/wordpress-shopify, https://xcler.dev/en/services/wordpress-development-germany and https://xcler.dev/en/services/shopify-development-germany. Service lead: Mehru Seemab.

## When XCLER recommends WordPress vs Shopify

- **WordPress / WooCommerce** when growth depends on organic visibility, thought-leadership content, many service pages and complex information architecture.
- **Shopify** when revenue runs through checkout and fulfilment, and reliable payments, logistics, ERP and CRM integrations matter most.
- **Headless** (Next.js front end on Shopify or a CMS) when a brand needs top performance plus editor comfort.

XCLER evaluates the business model, catalogue complexity and team operations before choosing.

## German market requirements XCLER covers

For shops in Germany, XCLER sets up GDPR-compliant consent, tax configuration including the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS), compliant invoicing (e.g. Lexoffice, sevDesk, DATEV export), German payment methods (PayPal, cards, Klarna, SEPA direct debit, purchase on invoice for B2B) and carriers such as DHL, DPD, Hermes and GLS.

## Migration and automation

XCLER plans migrations between platforms with data mapping, redirects and tracking continuity to protect SEO and conversions. Storefronts can be connected to n8n, Make.com and AI chatbots for lead routing, customer support and operations.
