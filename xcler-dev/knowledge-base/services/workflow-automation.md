## What XCLER's workflow automation service is

XCLER works as an n8n agency and Make.com agency. XCLER builds workflow automation that removes repetitive handoffs between tools — syncing CRM, messaging, inboxes, billing and operations — and replaces fragile spreadsheets and manual copy-paste. ## Which automation tools XCLER uses

- **Make.com:** multi-step workflows across sales, marketing, support and operations; best for fast SaaS glue.
- **n8n (including self-hosted):** for businesses that need control, data ownership, self-hosting and denser logic.
- **Zapier:** fast, reliable automations for standard business scenarios.
- **GoHighLevel:** CRM and lifecycle automations including pipelines, messaging and booking sequences.

XCLER chooses the tool based on complexity, hosting preferences, compliance, volume and how tightly AI agents need to integrate.

## How XCLER builds reliable workflows

- **API-centric orchestration:** systems connect through stable interfaces and explicit triggers, so workflows stay testable and traceable.
- **Governance and rollback:** automations are versioned, validated and documented with clear failure paths.
- **Idempotent steps, retries and alerting:** failures are visible and recoverable.
- **SLA-oriented monitoring:** runtime, failure rates and important events are tracked in real time.
- **AI-augmented decisions:** where useful, AI classification, routing and prioritisation are added, with guardrails and human escalation.
- **Runbooks:** every scenario is documented so the client's team owns the system after launch.

## Workflow vs process automation

Workflow automation wires tools together (CRM, inbox, billing). Process automation models the complete operating flow, including ownership, retries and escalation. XCLER delivers both.

## When to choose n8n vs Make.com

Make.com fits fast SaaS glue and visual scenarios. n8n fits self-hosting, data control, high volume and denser logic, including native AI agent and vector-store nodes. XCLER recommends the stack based on compliance, volume and team ownership. A detailed comparison is on the blog: https://xcler.dev/en/blog/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier-2026.
